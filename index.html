About

Hi, I am Dipti Chhatrapati

Enterprise AI Architect and Delivery Leader with 15+ years of experience across Fortune-scale organizations, designing governance frameworks, leading large-scale platform transformations, and building agentic AI solutions.

  • Presales & Field Enablement: Supported enterprise presales, revenue enablement, and Microsoft field engagement through solution positioning and direct collaboration with Microsoft product leadership.
